About

Practical guidance for builders who want visible results.

Samuel Katumba is a Zambian entrepreneur, university lecturer, and digital skills trainer based in Kitwe. He holds a Bachelor of Science in Computer Science from the University of the People (USA), a Bachelor of Science in Tourism Management from Amity University (India), and is currently pursuing a Master of Computer Application in Artificial Intelligence at Amity University (India). He also holds a Postgraduate Diploma in Teaching Methodology from Copperstone University. He has built multiple commercial-level projects, some of which are showcased on this page.

Once upon a time, building a website or mobile app required years of university training, expensive courses, and countless hours of practice. That world no longer exists. Today, with the power of AI, anyone — regardless of background or experience — can build, launch, and maintain professional websites and mobile applications in just ONE DAY. University degrees still matter, but they are no longer the only path to a successful tech career. AI is the great equalizer. Samuel believes the AI revolution has made web development accessible to everyone — and he's on a mission to train 10,000 Zambians for FREE.
